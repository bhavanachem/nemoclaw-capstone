## Working definition of a spike
A spike is a reading or sustained period where:
- Magnitude: significantly exceeds the per-meter P99 for that hour-of-day, or exceeds P50 by a large multiple (e.g. 5x+), especially if sustained across multiple consecutive readings
- Duration: more than a single isolated reading (appliance cycles are brief; sustained elevation is suspicious)
- Time of day: occurs during normally low-load hours AND is large relative to that hour's baseline
- Multi-meter: several meters move together (suggests whole-house event, not single appliance)

Ordinary non-spike peaks: routine appliance cycles (oven, dryer) that are brief and within the meter's historical P99/max range.
